# The Consortium Rooftop — opening movie production plan

Placement: before Varenholm Gate quest 1, before its chapter card and Tesfaye's first conversation. Approximate edited length: 35–40 seconds, landscape 16:9, continuous animated bodies, H.264/AAC MP4 with `faststart` and a mobile-friendly bitrate. The final duration follows the performances and the long fall; there is no ad-length constraint on this movie.

This is an original scene in Varenholm, inspired by the requested premise. Use the game's approved anime visual style and Kolade's existing black spiked plate and closed helmet. Do not reveal his face or civilian identity. The unknown victim is an adult; he is not Hiwot, Tesfaye, the protagonist, or a previously recruited companion.

## Shots

1. **0–7 seconds — the roof.** A rain-soaked Consortium tower overlooks the city at night. Track at human height behind a frightened man stumbling across the tiles. He looks back, loses his footing, catches himself and scrambles toward a door. The armoured pursuer steps into his path. Both figures visibly move through the environment; this must not be an animated photograph.
2. **7–19 seconds — the plea.** In an uninterrupted medium-wide view, the victim backs toward the parapet with both hands raised. Kolade advances and catches the front of his coat. The victim struggles, twists and tries to brace his boots. Keep the closed helmet and body proportions consistent. Cut closer only after the full-body action is established.
3. **19–25 seconds — the throw.** Side view shows Kolade haul him off his feet and over the parapet, then release him outward. Follow the victim's body past the roof edge. The action must connect visibly and anatomically: no disappearing grip, morphing hands, substituted body or teleportation between shots.
4. **25–34 seconds — the fall.** Track down the tower wall with the falling man. Successive floors and windows pass quickly enough to establish a very long fall. His limbs and coat move with gravity and air resistance. Reveal the iron fence below, show the impact and resulting impalement briefly at a readable distance, and cut away without lingering on the injury.
5. **34–40 seconds — aftermath.** Return to the roof. A thin supernatural light rises toward the armoured figure and is absorbed. Kolade lowers his hand, turns and walks back toward the door. End on black, then the game's prologue card.
